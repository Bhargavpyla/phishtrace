# Choosing a disposition
Propose create_incident (likely_phishing) only when at least one strong technical indicator is cited, such as a link text mismatch, reply-to mismatch, lookalike domain, executable attachment or instruction-like text. Propose route_manual_investigation (needs_investigation) when signals conflict, evidence is missing, or a benign explanation is plausible but unconfirmed. Propose reviewed_closure (no_strong_indicators) only when no strong indicators were found and the reported items were checked; this closes the report after human review and never declares the email safe. Every proposal needs a human reviewer. List what could not be established as unknowns.
